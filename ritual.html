The table is set. The rest is yours.

A long lunch. An impromptu toast. A quiet hour at the end of the day. No special occasion required.

01 Chill & uncork +

Chill well. Open the cork and cage carefully, then let the moment begin.

02 Pour & savour +

Choose a wine glass or flute to enjoy the aroma and fine bubbles.

03 Find your pairing +

Bring to the table with seafood, cheeses, or fruit desserts. Or simply enjoy a glass on its own.

Pour with a little room to linger.

Put the bottle in the fridge ahead of your guests arriving. Set out wine glasses or flutes and open the cork carefully when you are ready to pour.

Start with a small glass so there is space to enjoy the aroma. Take a sip before the food arrives, then return to it with the first bite. Keep the bottle chilled between pours.

For a relaxed tasting, choose two teas and serve them in separate glasses. Let everyone find their favourite before bringing out the next course.

Let the meal set the pace.

For a long lunch, bring the bottle out with the first plates and let guests top up as the meal unfolds. At a larger gathering, set it beside the other drinks so everyone can choose their own glass.

A cheese board or a few small dishes make an easy setting for comparing teas. You can also save a glass to try with dessert, returning to the same tea at a different point in the meal.
