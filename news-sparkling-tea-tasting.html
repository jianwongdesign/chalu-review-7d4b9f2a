Host a sparkling tea tasting.

A few bottles, clear glasses and something good to eat. Make an evening of discovering your favourite expression.

Sample editorial story for this design preview.

Set out two different teas

A small tasting is easy to build around two expressions. Choose bottles with contrasting descriptions so there is something clear to compare. You could begin with Snow Jasmine and Volcano Oolong, then explore the other teas another evening.

Chill the bottles ahead of time. Give each tea its own glass and leave enough room on the table for a few plates of food. Open the corks carefully when everyone is ready.

Take your time with the first sip

Start with a small pour. Notice the colour and aroma before tasting, then give yourself a moment before reaching for the second glass. You can write down a few impressions, or simply talk about what you enjoy.

Return to the first tea after tasting the second. The comparison may draw your attention to a different part of the flavour. Keep the bottles chilled between pours.

Add a little food

Bring out a familiar dish and taste again. A few rice-paper rolls, roasted mushrooms or a cheese board can give everyone something to try alongside their glass.

Use the product pages for pairing ideas, then follow your own preferences. At the end, ask everyone which tea they would choose for their next meal. That gives you an easy starting point for another gathering.
