The perfect pour +

Serve well chilled in a wine glass or flute. Open the cork carefully and pour slowly, leaving space in the glass for the aroma. Start with a taste on its own, then return to the glass with something savoury from the table.

Get to know Hibiscus Noir.

Take a moment with the colour and aroma before your first sip. Hibiscus Noir invites a closer look at the fruit-led side of the collection, especially when you have a richer dish on the table.

Compare a small glass on its own with a sip after food. Try roast duck for one meal, then an earthy mushroom dish for another. The suggested pairings below are different ways to explore the same tea, rather than a fixed menu you need to follow.

At your table

Bring it out for dinner with friends, or open a bottle when you lay out a cheese board. Serve a small first glass and let everyone choose a bite to try with it. It also makes an interesting side-by-side tasting with the lighter Snow Jasmine.
