The perfect pour +

Chill the bottle well and open the cork carefully. Pour a little into a wine glass or flute, leaving room to appreciate the aroma. Taste before adding any garnish, then try a sip alongside the first bite of your meal. Keep the bottle chilled between pours.

Get to know Snow Jasmine.

Begin with the aroma before taking a sip. Snow Jasmine is a good place to explore the lighter side of the collection: follow the floral note first, then look for the gentle fruit character described in the tasting notes above.

Try your first glass on its own, then take another sip with a lightly flavoured dish. A little fresh ginger or a few herbs can give you a different way into the tea. Keep sauces gentle for your first pairing so you can still notice what is happening in the glass.

At your table

Pour it as guests arrive for lunch, or set it beside a plate of rice-paper rolls for an easy starter. If you are tasting several Chalu teas, begin here before moving on to a more toasted or structured expression.
