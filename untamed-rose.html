The perfect pour +

Serve well chilled in a wine glass or flute. Open the cork carefully and begin with a small pour so there is room to enjoy the aroma. Keep the bottle chilled as lunch unfolds and taste again alongside a fresh or fruity dish.

Get to know Untamed Rosé.

Begin with the aroma, then follow the finish after your first sip. Untamed Rosé brings several tasting cues to explore, so take a moment to notice which one stands out to you before looking back at the notes above.

Try it first with a fresh dish, such as tomato and burrata, then revisit the glass with a bite of fruit. Those two combinations give you different ways to explore the same pour. The suggested dishes below are an easy place to start your own comparison.

At your table

Choose it for a lunch that carries on into the afternoon, or a table of smaller dishes passed around to share. Save a little to try with a strawberry tart at the end of the meal and see how your impression changes.
