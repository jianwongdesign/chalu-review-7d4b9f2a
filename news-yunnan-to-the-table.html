From Yunnan to the table.

Meet the ideas behind Chalu, from a shared curiosity about tea to a bottle made for gathering around the table.

Sample editorial story for this design preview.

A shared beginning

In 2019, Mia and Fraser moved from Shanghai to Yunnan. Mia brought her interest in tea; Fraser brought his brewing experience. Their work together became Chalu, an alcohol-free sparkling tea named after the Tea Horse Road.

That background offers a useful starting point for exploring the collection. Look at the tea and origin on each bottle’s page, then turn to the tasting notes to see what you might notice in the glass.

Begin with one bottle

Choose a tea that sounds like something you would enjoy with dinner. There is plenty to explore in a single bottle: the aroma before your first sip, the bubbles, and the impression that stays afterwards.

Invite someone else to taste with you. Compare what you notice before looking back at the flavour descriptions. Different impressions can make the conversation as interesting as the pairing.

Bring it to your own table

Keep the first meal simple. A familiar dish gives you an easy way to notice what the tea brings to the combination. Try another sip after a bite, then decide what you would pair it with next time.

The four product pages include individual tasting notes and suggested dishes. Use them to plan a small tasting, or choose a bottle to open at your next lunch together.
