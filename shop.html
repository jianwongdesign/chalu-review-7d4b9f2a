Find the tea for your table.

Start with the flavours you already enjoy. A floral drink can sit comfortably beside a light lunch; a drier, toasted tea can carry through a savoury main course. Each Chalu expression has its own page, with a closer look at the blend and ideas for what to serve alongside it.

Choosing for a group? Set two different teas on the table and let everyone compare. Try a bright pour with the first course, then bring out something deeper when the food arrives.

For a light first glass

Start with Snow Jasmine if you enjoy a gentle floral character. Try it with fresh herbs, a softly flavoured starter or a fruit dessert. It is an easy place to begin a tasting of the collection.

Explore Snow Jasmine ↗

For a richer plate

Bring Hibiscus Noir to the table when the food has more depth. Our pairing suggestions include roast duck and mushroom risotto, with a cheese board for an evening spent sharing small plates.

Explore Hibiscus Noir ↗

For savoury flavours

Choose Volcano Oolong for a grilled or roasted main course. Try a small sip before a bite of food, then another afterwards to notice how the tea and the dish change each other.

Explore Volcano Oolong ↗

For a bright lunch

Take Untamed Rosé to a table of salmon, tomato salad or summer fruit. Keep the bottle chilled while lunch unfolds and give everyone a little room in the glass to enjoy the aroma.

Explore Untamed Rosé ↗

Make a tasting of it.

Pour a small amount of each tea into separate glasses. Notice the colour first, then the aroma, before taking a sip. Return to your favourite with a bite of food and see which combination you would serve again.

The dish suggestions here are starting points for your own table. Browse the individual pages to compare flavours, or visit The Ritual for serving guidance.
